Hello, Cricut.

The hologram that plays inside the box. Copy, icons and photos are stand-ins until the real ones drop in.

How to test

  1. Brightness all the way up, and Auto-Lock off if it dims early.
  2. Open the box and make sure the acetate sits at its 45° angle.
  3. Tap Test pattern, lay the phone face-down over the top window, and look in through the front. Change the settings until “READ ME” reads correctly and “BACK” points up.
  4. Tap Play. You get a countdown to place the phone, then the video plays.
